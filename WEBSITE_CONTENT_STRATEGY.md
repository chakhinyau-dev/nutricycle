### Page 2: Features (`/features`)

**Purpose:** Detailed breakdown of every feature for users doing research.

#### Section 1 — Cycle Intelligence
- **Cycle Setup Wizard:** Enter your last period date, cycle length, and health goal. Takes 2 minutes.
- **Smart Cycle Calendar:** Color-coded phases with fertile window, period days, and daily log dots.
- **Period Calculator:** Predict your next period and fertile window at any time.
- **Hormone Chart:** Animated SVG chart showing estrogen and progesterone levels across your full cycle with your current day marked.

#### Section 2 — Nutrition & Recipes
- **Phase-Based Recipes:** Every recipe is tagged to a cycle phase (menstrual, follicular, ovulation, luteal) and meal type (breakfast, lunch, snack, dinner).
- **Weekly Meal Planner:** Auto-generates a 7-day plan matched to your current phase. Macro breakdown (protein, carbs, fat) per meal.
- **Key Foods Guide:** Browse hormone-supporting foods by phase with tags: estrogen, progesterone, anti-inflammatory, energy.
- **Shopping List:** Auto-built from your phase foods and recipe plan. Add custom items. Check off as you shop.

- **NutriCycle AI Chat:** Ask anything about your cycle, symptoms, or nutrition. The AI knows your current phase and cycle day, so answers are personalized. Powered by Google Gemini 2.0.

#### Section 4 — Daily Tracking
- **Daily Log:** Record symptoms, mood (4 levels), energy (3 levels), and custom notes. Review history anytime.
- **Hydration Tracker:** Log daily water intake with quick-add shortcuts (glass, bottle, thermos).
- **Wellness Activities:** Guided yoga, meditation, and breathing routines with a built-in countdown timer. YouTube videos included.

#### Section 5 — Content & Learning
- **Article Library:** Evidence-based articles on cycle health and nutrition. Auto-translated to your language.
- **Video Library:** Phase-specific wellness videos from YouTube + exclusive uploads. Search and filter by phase.

Enter your last period date and cycle length. NutriCycle calculates your current phase instantly.

Your dashboard shows your hormone chart, today's phase, recommended foods, and a weekly meal plan — all tailored to where you are in your cycle.

#### Step 4 — Log, learn, and adapt
Log daily symptoms and energy. Chat with the AI. Try phase-specific recipes and wellness activities. The more you log, the more personalized your experience becomes.

#### Step 5 — Track your progress
Review your cycle history on the calendar. See patterns in your mood and energy across months.

## 3. Trivia & Educational Content Bank

These facts can be used across the blog, social media, and app onboarding.

### Menstrual Cycle Trivia

- The average menstrual cycle is **28 days**, but a healthy cycle can range from 21 to 35 days.
- The menstrual cycle has **4 distinct phases**: menstrual, follicular, ovulation, and luteal — each with different hormonal profiles and nutritional needs.
- **Estrogen peaks** just before ovulation, causing a natural energy and mood boost for many women.
- **Progesterone rises** in the luteal phase and is responsible for PMS symptoms like bloating, cravings, and fatigue.
- Women lose on average **30–80ml of blood** during menstruation — iron-rich foods like lentils, spinach, and red meat help replenish it.
- The **follicular phase** (days 1–13) is when the body has the most capacity for new learning, physical training, and social connection.
- **Ovulation lasts only 24 hours**, but sperm can survive up to 5 days — creating a fertile window of approximately 6 days.
- The **luteal phase** (days 15–28) raises the body's resting metabolic rate by up to 300 calories per day, which partly explains pre-period hunger.
- **Magnesium** deficiency is linked to stronger PMS symptoms. Foods rich in magnesium: dark chocolate, almonds, spinach, and avocado.
- Cycle length and symptoms can be affected by stress, sleep, travel, and significant weight changes.

### Nutrition & Cycle Science

- **Menstrual phase:** Iron, magnesium, and omega-3s help with cramps and blood loss. Warm, cooked foods are easier to digest.
- **Follicular phase:** Light, fresh foods support rising estrogen. Fermented foods (yogurt, kimchi) help with estrogen metabolism.
- **Ovulation phase:** Antioxidant-rich foods (berries, leafy greens) support egg health. Raw vegetables are well-tolerated at peak energy.
- **Luteal phase:** Complex carbohydrates (oats, sweet potato, quinoa) stabilize blood sugar and reduce cravings. B6 (found in bananas and chickpeas) supports progesterone production.
- **Caffeine** can worsen PMS symptoms and breast tenderness for some women — especially in the luteal phase.
- **Seed cycling** is a practice of eating specific seeds in each phase: flax + pumpkin (follicular) and sesame + sunflower (luteal) to support hormone balance.
- **Zinc** (found in pumpkin seeds, beef, shellfish) supports ovulation and may reduce period pain.
- **Vitamin D** deficiency has been linked to more severe PMS symptoms and irregular cycles.

### Wellness Trivia

- **Exercise tolerance changes** across the cycle — strength peaks in the follicular and ovulation phases; lighter movement (yoga, walking) is better tolerated in the late luteal and menstrual phases.
- **Sleep quality** often declines in the late luteal phase due to rising progesterone and body temperature.
- **Stress** raises cortisol, which suppresses progesterone and can delay ovulation or shorten the luteal phase.
- Hydration needs **increase slightly** before and during menstruation due to prostaglandins causing fluid shifts.
- **Gut health** is closely linked to estrogen metabolism — a diverse microbiome helps prevent estrogen dominance.

## 5. Visual & Design Direction

### Color Palette (from the app)
- **Primary:** Sage green `#A3B3A5`
- **Menstrual:** Soft rose `#E8A0A2`
- **Follicular:** Mint green `#B8D8BC`
- **Ovulation:** Warm gold `#F9E4B7`
- **Luteal:** Soft lavender `#C8BCE0`
- **Text:** Deep charcoal `#2C2C2C`

### Typography Direction
- Display / headings: Serif typeface (elegant, trustworthy — matches app's Instrument Serif)
- Body: Clean sans-serif (readable at all sizes — matches app's Outfit)
- Data labels: Monospace or tabular figures

### Image Direction
- Women in natural light, diverse ages 20–40
- Food photography: vibrant, fresh, phase-appropriate colors
- App mockups: iPhone and Android device frames
- Avoid: medical/clinical imagery, blue tones, stock clichés

### Page Layout Principles
- Mobile-first (most users will arrive from Instagram or TikTok on mobile)
- Hero above the fold with download CTA
- Feature sections alternate text-left / image-right
- Phase color chips used as visual anchors throughout
- Minimal nav: Logo · Features · Membership · Blog · Download
