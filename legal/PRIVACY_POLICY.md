## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Account information
When you create an account, we (through our authentication provider, Clerk) collect your **email address**, and optionally your **name** and **profile photo**. You may sign up with an email/password, Google, or Apple Sign-In.

### 1.2 Cycle and health information
To provide the App's core features, we collect information you choose to enter, including:
- Cycle data: last period start date, average cycle length, average period length, and your stated wellness goal
- Daily logs: mood, physical symptoms (e.g., cramps, headache, insomnia), and free-text notes you write
- Hydration and shopping-list activity you record in the App

This information is used solely to calculate your estimated cycle phase, generate personalized nutrition/wellness content, and power the App's AI features described below. **We do not use this data for advertising, and we do not sell it.**

### 1.3 AI chat and predictions
If you use the AI Chat or AI Predictor features, your messages and relevant cycle/symptom context are sent to **Google's Gemini API** to generate a response. Google's own privacy terms govern how that data is processed on their end; see [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). AI-generated responses are informational only — see the Terms of Use for the full disclaimer.

### 1.4 Subscription and payment information
Subscriptions are sold and billed through the **Apple App Store** or **Google Play Store**. We never see or store your payment card details. Purchase and billing management is handled by **RevenueCat**, which shares your subscription status (active, canceled, plan type, renewal date) with us so the App can unlock Premium features. See [RevenueCat's Privacy Policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy) and Apple's/Google's own privacy policies for how they handle payment data.

### 1.5 Technical information
We may collect basic technical information needed to operate the App, such as your device's operating system and app version, for troubleshooting purposes.

### 1.6 Notifications
The App may request permission to send local reminders (e.g., hydration reminders, phase-change alerts). These are generated and scheduled on your device — as of this version, we do not operate a push-notification server, so notification content never leaves your device.

We use the information above to:

## 3. Third Parties We Work With

| Service | Purpose | What's shared |
|---|---|---|
| **Clerk** | Authentication / account management | Email, name, authentication credentials |
| **Supabase** | Database and file storage | Cycle data, daily logs, saved recipes, profile info |
| **RevenueCat** | Subscription management | Purchase/subscription status |

## 4. Data Retention & Account Deletion

You can permanently delete your account and associated data at any time from **Settings → Delete Account** in the App. When you do, we delete your profile, cycle data, daily logs, saved recipes, and subscription record from our database, and your login credentials are removed from our authentication provider.

Please note: Apple, Google, and RevenueCat may independently retain transaction records as required for tax, accounting, or legal purposes, and this is outside our control.
