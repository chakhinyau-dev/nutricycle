# Eliminar tu cuenta de NutriCycle / Delete your NutriCycle account

> Publish this as its own simple, static page (e.g. `aliciabasurto.com/eliminar-cuenta`) — deliberately NOT dependent on the same Next.js app that's been intermittently down. This is the URL to put in Google Play Console's Data Safety → Account Deletion field.

## Español

Puedes eliminar tu cuenta de NutriCycle y todos tus datos asociados (perfil, historial de ciclo, registros diarios, recetas guardadas, suscripción) de dos maneras:

**Opción 1 — Directamente en la app (recomendado, inmediato)**
1. Abre la app NutriCycle
2. Ve a **Ajustes → Eliminar Cuenta**
3. Confirma la eliminación

Esta acción es permanente y no se puede deshacer.

**Opción 2 — Si ya no tienes acceso a la app**
Envía un correo a **hola@aliciabasurto.com** desde el email asociado a tu cuenta, solicitando la eliminación. Procesaremos tu solicitud dentro de los 30 días siguientes.

---

## English

You can delete your NutriCycle account and all associated data (profile, cycle history, daily logs, saved recipes, subscription record) in two ways:

**Option 1 — Directly in the app (recommended, immediate)**
1. Open the NutriCycle app
2. Go to **Settings → Delete Account**
3. Confirm deletion

This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

**Option 2 — If you no longer have access to the app**
Email **hola@aliciabasurto.com** from the address associated with your account, requesting deletion. We will process your request within 30 days.
